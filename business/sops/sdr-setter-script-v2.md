# Setter Script v2 — 2026-04-23

Rewrite of the old `CG_Setter_&_Closing_Script`. Honest about the actual offer so the handoff to the closer doesn't break trust.

**Actual offer (quote these numbers only):**
- $1,000 setup
- $150 per booked appointment
- $44/day ad spend, paid by client direct to the ad platform

**Goal of this call:** Qualify, create pain, earn the right to book a 30-minute deep-dive with the closer. **Do not quote price on this call** — the closer handles the full pitch + price.

---

## Pre-call

- Fathom is running and recording BEFORE the call starts. No exceptions.
- Camera on, professional background, quiet environment.
- Booking link pulled up: **use YOUR OWN link from the index doc** — Jay, Alex, Jowanna each have their own.

---

## 1. Open

Act as the business owner, not an assistant. Use your real name. It raises show rates.

> *"Hey, did you see the message my assistant Alice sent over about getting you more jobs? This is [Your Name] from Contractor Growth."*

**If they don't remember:**
> *"No worries — quick question, would getting more qualified estimates in your area be something relevant for your business right now?"*

YES → continue. NO → thank them, end the call.

---

## 2. Mini-pitch (30 seconds)

> *"So at a high level — we run lead-gen specifically for contractors. We generate estimate requests in your service area, qualify them so you're not wasting time on tire-kickers, and book them straight onto your calendar. We only work with one contractor per 30-mile radius so you're not competing with anyone we send.
>
> Does that sound like something worth 5 more minutes to see if it's a fit?"*

Wait for YES. If NO → end the call.

---

## 3. Frame

> *"Here's how this call goes — I'll ask you a few questions about the business. If it looks like a fit, we book you a 30-minute call with me [or: with our team] where we'll walk you through exactly how the system works, the numbers, and what getting started looks like. Sound good?"*

**Do NOT say "pay on results" or "only pay for what closes." That is not our offer. If asked directly about price here, use the handle in the objections section.**

---

## 4. Discovery (diagnose, don't survey — follow up every answer with "why")

### Q1 — Current state
> *"How's business right now — are you slammed or do you have crews sitting around waiting for work?"*

### Q2 — Desired state
> *"How many more jobs a month could you realistically take on without breaking your operation?"*

### Q3 — Motivation (most important — write their exact words)
> *"If we handed you those extra [X] jobs a month, what would that actually do for the business? Hire another crew? New truck? Keep the guys busy through the slow season?"*

### Q4 — Trade + service area
> *"And what trade are you in — and what's your service area, like what radius do you cover?"*

Ask service area, not where they're based.

### Q5 — Marketing history
> *"Have you tried any marketing before — Angie, HomeAdvisor, Yelp, Facebook ads, anything?"*

**If YES:** *"How did that go — what was the biggest headache?"* Let them vent. Use a rebuttal from the objection section.

**If NO / good experience:** *"That's great — we can help you scale what works."*

---

## 5. The book ask

> *"Honestly, based on what you just told me, I'm pretty confident we can help. The way we take this forward is a 30-minute call where I walk you through the system — how we generate the estimates, how we qualify them, and what the numbers look like for a business your size. You'll leave that call knowing whether it's a fit or not. Fair enough?"*

Wait for YES.

---

## 6. Book (assume the close)

Never ask "would you like to book?" Move straight to logistics.

**Email:**
> *"Perfect. What's the best email to send everything over to?"*

**Timezone + time:**
> *"What timezone are you in?"*
> *"I've got [Time A] or [Time B] — which works better?"*

**Commitment:**
> *"My schedule's packed — if I lock this in, can I get your 100% guarantee you'll be there ready to go?"*

Wait for "yes I'll be there."

**Save contact:**
> *"One more thing — save my number in your phone as 'More Jobs.' That way when I call 30 minutes before, you know it's me."*

**Close:**
> *"Great talking to you — I'm [Your Name], the business owner. Looking forward to [time]."*

---

## Objection handles (setter-stage only)

### "How much does it cost?"
> *"Totally fair question — and I could throw a number at you right now, but it wouldn't be honest. The actual investment depends on your service area, ad budget, and how many jobs a month you want. That's exactly why we do the 30-minute call — so we can show you the math for YOUR business, not generic numbers that won't apply. Fair enough?"*

**Do NOT say "we only charge on results" or "nothing upfront." That's not our offer and will break trust when the closer pitches the real price.**

### "Is this pay-per-job?" / "Am I only paying when I close deals?"
> *"Not exactly — we're a pay-per-appointment model, not pay-per-closed-deal. We generate and qualify the estimate requests, you pay for each one that gets booked onto your calendar. The closer will walk you through the full structure on the 30-minute call."*

### "Is this like Yelp or Angie's?"
> *"Not at all. Those platforms sell the same lead to 5 contractors — you're competing before you pick up the phone. We only work with one contractor per 30-mile radius, so every estimate we send is yours alone."*

### "Send me some info first"
> *"Totally get that. Problem is, a generic PDF won't tell you whether this works for YOUR service area or YOUR revenue goals. The 30-minute call is literally that info — just custom to your business. Which time works — [Time A] or [Time B]?"*

### "I need to think about it" (on the book ask)
> *"Appreciate that — quick question though, is there something specific that's making you hesitate? Because what we'd cover on that call is literally the info that would help you decide."*

If they give a real objection → handle it. If they just want off the call → let them go with a short follow-up agreement ("I'll shoot you one more note next week — if the timing's better, we'll grab a slot then").

---

## After the call

- Log outcome in the tracking sheet immediately (show rate depends on you logging BEFORE the next call).
- Flag any promises you made (timing, service area, anything specific) in Fathom notes so the closer doesn't contradict you.
- If you mentioned any price or offer detail, note the exact words. The closer needs to know what frame you set.

---

## What will break this whole system

1. Saying "pay on results" / "nothing upfront" — closer will look like a liar when they open with $1,000 setup.
2. Quoting an ad spend number other than $44/day minimum.
3. Promising an exclusivity radius bigger or smaller than 30 miles.
4. Using someone else's booking link.
5. Not recording in Fathom.

If any of these happen on your call, flag it in your end-of-day report. We'd rather catch it early than let it tank the closer call.
