# SDR Call Review Rubric

**Purpose:** Score every reviewed SDR call against the Contractor Growth script. Built for speed (under 5 minutes per call) and accountability (evidence-based coaching, not opinion).

**Source script:** [CG Setter & Closing Script](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZCuFmB7aIqJNg-P-92YDtcfhdgTZDCvhTotpvO6r0-I/edit)
**Source framework:** Matt Ryder sales course (distilled in `business/knowledge-hq/`) + Jack M's full-cycle standard

---

## How to use

1. Pull the GHL recording (or transcript). Note the SDR name, prospect name, date, and call length.
2. Go top to bottom. Check off each item that happened. Leave blank if it didn't.
3. Score each section 1-5 based on execution quality (not just presence).
4. Total the score. Write 3 coaching notes max — specific quotes + timestamps.
5. Drop the scorecard into Notion + weekly review folder.

**Scoring scale (applies to every 1-5 section):**
- **5** — nailed it, script-perfect, calm authority
- **4** — hit the moment, minor drift
- **3** — partial execution, some pieces missing
- **2** — attempted but off-script or reactive
- **1** — skipped or botched

**Max total: 50 points across 10 sections.** Under 30 = critical coaching needed. 30-39 = targeted coaching. 40+ = reps who can teach others.

## Pre-call checks (PASS / FAIL — any fail is a coaching flag)

- [ ] GHL recording was on from call start
- [ ] SDR had prospect's notes/niche/name ready before dialing
- [ ] No background noise, interruptions, or unprofessional setting audible

---

## 1. Open & 30-second pitch (/5)

- [ ] Used the "did you see the message my assistant Alice sent over" opener (or equivalent name-of-assistant framing — SDR presents as the owner, not an assistant)
- [ ] If prospect didn't remember: asked "would a pay-per-job be something relevant for your business?"
- [ ] Delivered the 30-second pitch covering: pay-on-results, no monthly subscription, app with name/address/needs, long-term partnership framing
- [ ] Waited for YES before continuing

**Score: __ /5**

---

## 2. Frame (/5)

- [ ] Set expectation: "I'm going to ask you a few questions, if it's a fit we'll book"
- [ ] Mentioned "leads as early as next day" (urgency anchor)
- [ ] Mentioned "only work with one contractor in a 30-mile radius" (exclusivity anchor)
- [ ] Tone was calm, authoritative — not salesy or scripted-sounding

**Score: __ /5**

---

## 3. KYC / Discovery questions (/5)

The four required questions. Each answered fully before moving to the next:

- [ ] **Q1 Current state:** "How's business going right now — slammed or crews sitting around?"
- [ ] **Q2 Desired state:** "How many more jobs a week could you realistically take on?"
- [ ] **Q3 Motivation:** "If we handed you those extra jobs — what would that actually do for the business?" (SDR let them talk, wrote down exact words)
- [ ] **Q4 Trade + Service area:** asked service area (radius), NOT where they're based
- [ ] SDR followed up every answer with WHY (diagnose, don't survey)

**Score: __ /5**

---

## 4. Marketing history / rebuttal setup (/5)

- [ ] Asked if they've worked with Angie's List, HomeAdvisor, Yelp, etc.
- [ ] If YES: asked "what was the biggest headache?" and let them vent
- [ ] Delivered rebuttal that differentiates CG from shared-lead platforms
- [ ] Did NOT argue with the prospect's past experience — validated first, then reframed

**Score: __ /5**

---

## 5. The 20-minute ask (/5)

This is the decision point — every call should attempt it before fallback to booking.

- [ ] Asked "do you have about 20 minutes?" BEFORE defaulting to booking
- [ ] Phrased it with confidence ("based on what you told me, I'm pretty confident we can help")
- [ ] If NO: moved cleanly to BOOK without pressure
- [ ] If YES: transitioned into deeper discovery, not straight into the pitch

**Score: __ /5**

---

## 6. Deeper discovery + future pace (/5) — only if the 20-min ask was YES

Skip this section if they went straight to booking. Note in the scorecard: "skipped — moved to book."

- [ ] Logical certainty: asked what they're doing now, how long, is it working
- [ ] Asked the turning point question: "what made you start questioning your current way of getting projects?"
- [ ] Drilled down with at least one of: "what do you mean by that," "how has that shown up," "how long has that been going on"
- [ ] Emotional certainty: asked why they want change, what's shifted, got something specific (not "I have more time now")
- [ ] Future pace: got at least 2 personal things (family time, truck, time off) — not just business goals

**Score: __ /5** (or N/A if skipped)

---

## 7. Pitch delivery (/5) — only if they pitched live

Skip this section if call ended at booking. Note: "booked, no live pitch."

- [ ] Shared screen with the Contractor Growth System Demo
- [ ] Walked through all 3 pillars: Visibility, Conversion, Credibility
- [ ] After each pillar: asked "can you see how that would help you specifically?"
- [ ] After full pitch: asked "do you feel like this would actually get you to [their goal]?"
- [ ] Followed with "why do you say that?" — did NOT let them off with a one-word answer

**Score: __ /5** (or N/A if skipped)

---

## 8. Objection handling (/5) — only if objections came up

Score the HARDEST objection the prospect raised. If no real objections, mark N/A.

The Matt Ryder framework: Parrot → Clarify → Guard drop → Isolate → Handle → Question-close.

- [ ] **Parrot:** repeated the objection back ("you want to think about it? When you say think about it, what do you mean?")
- [ ] **Clarify:** got to the real objection underneath
- [ ] **Guard drop:** delivered the calming line ("oh got you, got you. Yeah, don't even worry about it")
- [ ] **Isolate:** asked "apart from that, is there anything else that would stop us moving forward?"
- [ ] **Handle:** logical reframe (preferred) or metaphor reframe
- [ ] Question-based close to finish: "what do you think is going to be the most intelligent decision from here?"
- [ ] Tone stayed slow, steady, calm — did NOT get defensive

**Score: __ /5** (or N/A)

---

## 9. Book / close execution (/5)

This is where deals are made or lost. Assume the close, don't ask.

- [ ] Did NOT say "would you like to book?" — moved straight into logistics
- [ ] Got email
- [ ] Got timezone FIRST, then converted before offering slots
- [ ] Got commitment language: "if I lock this in, can I get your 100% guarantee you'll be there?"
- [ ] Got prospect to save their number as "More Jobs" in their phone
- [ ] Closed with "by the way, I'm [Name], the business owner" (reframe revelation)

**Score: __ /5**

---

## 10. Tonality & authority (/5)

The one thing that determines close rate more than words.

- [ ] Slow pace — did NOT rush, fill silence with nervous talking, or step on the prospect
- [ ] Steady tone — no voice cracks, no over-eagerness, no defensive snap
- [ ] Calm like talking to a friend, not pitching a stranger
- [ ] Outcome-independent — did NOT sound desperate or like they needed the deal
- [ ] Listened more than talked in discovery (target: prospect talks 70%+ during KYC)

**Score: __ /5**

---

## Post-call checks (PASS / FAIL)

- [ ] SDR logged outcome in the GHL appointments sheet (closed / follow-up / DQ)
- [ ] SDR sent EOD summary if this was their last call of the day

## Weekly roll-up (done in the SDR performance tab of the tracking sheet)

For each SDR, weekly:
- Avg score across calls reviewed
- Lowest-scoring section (where to coach)
- Trend vs prior week (improving / flat / declining)
- Hit rate on the three fireable offenses: recording on, outcome logged, script used

---

## Notes on the rubric itself

- This rubric intentionally mirrors the Contractor Growth Setter/Closing Script section-by-section. If the script changes, update this file.
- Matt Ryder's framework is the underlying theory; the script is the specific implementation. Score adherence to the script, coach with the framework.
- Do not score on gut feel — every check should map to an observable moment in the recording. If you can't point to the timestamp, don't check the box.
