## Frameworks & Principles

- **The Reset Call** — When a client is texting too much with petty complaints, call them directly. Say "I'm seeing a lot of text messages, lots of problems — talk to me, what can I do to help?" Then walk them through the actual metrics (leads, appointments, bookings) so they see the real picture. Reasserts frame and calms them down. Apply when client is panicking or blaming.
- **Inject Certainty** — On sales calls, your tonality and word choice must convey "this is what we do, this works, this is our process." Uncertainty in your voice kills deals. Especially needed during rebuttals — have pre-loaded responses, not on-the-fly thinking.
- **The Disinterested Tone (for SMS setters / cold callers)** — Arrogant, cocky, speedy, snappy. You should want to be off the phone MORE than the prospect does. Interrupt, push forward, create momentum. Opposite of the cheerful telemarketer. "A guy on a f***ing mission."
- **Don't Dig Personal Pain** — Do NOT ask B2C-style "how is this affecting you personally" questions on B2B sales calls. 90% of people won't answer; the 10% who do will start crying. Business impact IS the personal impact — ask about the business, that's already personal for the owner.
- **Individual [[ad spend]], Not Campaign Total** — Stop looking at campaign total ($70 total, no leads = panic). Look at spend per individual ad. That's the true indicator of whether something is on track.
- **Meta Budget Floor** — You cannot split a low budget across multiple ad sets. If total is $33, put all creatives in ONE ad set. Meta struggles to deliver quality audiences on $11 per ad set. Give it concentrated spend to find good audiences.
- **Seasoned Pixel = Speed to Results** — A pixel with years of training data in an account means leads/bookings come almost instantly when you launch. Pixels belong to the business portfolio, not the ad account — you can reassign one pixel across multiple ad accounts.
- **Always Multiple Ad Accounts** — Never rely on one. Assign the same pixel across them.
- **Eyeline Caption Rule** — ~50% of video ad views happen without audio. Captions must be at/near the eyeline (center-screen vertically), not at the bottom where they get covered. CapCut "Emoji" captions in the glow section are the preferred style.
- **Only Change What Isn't Working** — If one ad creative underperforms and others print, only swap the underperformer. Don't rewrite winning creatives.
- **Double-Down by Duplication, Not Spend Concentration** — To scale a winning ad, duplicate the ad set with a slightly different body. Don't dump all the budget into one ad set — it interrupts the winner's learning.
- **Love the Chaos** — Stress tolerance only increases by pushing the boundary harder. The lifestyle IS back-to-back, loads to do, minimal sleep. That's what separates operators who make more money.
- **Ignore Low-Value Client Texts** — If a client texts "that lead was out of state" or "they didn't answer," ignore it. Only respond to messages with data or real questions. They're venting frustration; don't absorb it.
- **Control the Assets** — Always keep ads, ad account, landing page under your control. Never give access. When clients get weird comments on ads or think something's off, they never see the backend.
- **Auto-Charge Offer** — All new clients going forward are on auto-charge (vs paying per appointment in advance). Harry is transitioning everyone new to this.

## Tactical Advice Given

### Pixel / Tracking Setup (Luca)
- In [[GoHighLevel]]: main funnel, edit landing page, top-left tracking code section. Paste Meta pixel code, change "PageView" to "Lead" (capital L) on landing page, "Schedule" on thank you page.
- If pixel was firing wrong event, the ad learning data is corrupted — duplicate the campaign and relaunch clean.
- Optimize Meta for the correct conversion event: website schedules, not leads, when you have a thank you page.
- B2C geography: United States + UK. Philippines for super high volume. South Africa decent. Egypt bad.

### Ad Set Structure (Sebastians)
- With $33 total budget and 3 creatives: all creatives in ONE ad set.
- Can only split into separate ad sets when budget per ad set is meaningfully large.

### Naming Convention (Raheem)
- Format: `NICHE // AS55 // CREATIVE TEST // 11MAR`
- AS55 = Ad Spend 55. All caps. Double slashes between sections.
- ABO (ad-set budget) for this style, not CBO.

### Campaign Build for B2B Acquisition
- Campaign → one ad set → multiple images/creatives. $55/day.
- Location: United States. Do NOT touch placements or targeting. Leave broad.
- Do NOT use the "limit reach" / frequency cap toggle for B2B — Meta's algorithm handles B2B better than B2C.
- Lead form → redirect to booking page (no need for full landing page + pixel initially).
- Headline = same text as on the ad creative (e.g., "$150,000 Qualified Appointment").
- CTA button: always "Learn More" (not "Book Now").

### Creative Copy
- Best-performing script right now = "Lean Mean Machine" (the one that talks about price at the start of the video) — specifically the "Mixed Quality Max Volume" variant inside it. Use the exact copy; don't substitute numbers or paraphrase.
- Don't show existing reactions/comments when duplicating ads — keeps the new creative clean and avoids carrying over mixed learning data.

### Sales / Setter Commission Structures
- **[[closer|Closers]]:** 10% per closed deal is standard.
- **Setters (Harry's aggressive model):** 30% of first-month client payment. Escalator: 3 closes → 30%, 5 → 40%, 7 → 45%. Works because front-end offer is low, so 10% would be "nothing."
- Accent matters for Filipino setters, but training/script is #1.
- Train setter by roleplay: "are you a marketing company?" → "I'm not a marketing guy. I'm a f***ing everything guy. I'm good at getting you new jobs."

### Cold-Calling Team
- Cheaper than SMS. Use same SMS script. Run on GoHighLevel. Pay per closed deal same as setters. Hire Filipinos via ads in Philippines.
- Run SMS with your best setter; put the rest on cold calling.

### Handling "Stolen Content" Complaint (Dan)
- If someone comments "quit using my content," tell the client it's a Facebook scam/troll — they message a lot, will ask for money. Reassures client instantly. Otherwise they demand creative changes you don't get paid enough for.

### When Metrics Look Bad Too Early
- $27-44 spent on one ad with no lead is NOT a signal. Need meaningful per-ad spend before deciding. Rule of thumb: "one more lead on a $85 spend drops CPL to $30-35."
