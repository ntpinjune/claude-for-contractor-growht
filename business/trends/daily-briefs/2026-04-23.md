- **Trigger**: Claude Opus 4.7 (98.5% vision accuracy) + Claude Design for polished output

- **Contractor fit**: Direct — roofing, HVAC, and painting contractors spend 1-3 hours per estimate; this is the highest-ROI time-save in the trades workflow
