## Weaknesses / what's killing her deals

Noah's review of 5 calls surfaced **4 objections she lost on**, all rooted in the same structural issue: no pain established before pitch. Listed by what's actually killing the most revenue first:

1. **No structure — prospect controls the frame.** Calls go: prospect asks price → she gives price → some questions → prospect asks more questions → she answers. There's no discovery, no pain, no flow to a pitch. This is the 80/20 fix.
2. **"What if I don't close the deal?"** — she answered with "trust, reputation, 5 years experience." Those are irrelevant authority claims the prospect can't verify. The right move is the **PGIHQ** framework + ROI math: ask their close rate, walk through "if we send 10, you close 5, at $X ticket, you'd net $Y," then question-close.
3. **"Can we just try one job first / commission only / X% of contract."** — she didn't probe. Right move: "Why specifically? Have you done that with other people?" Get them talking. Then reframe with the model that protects them better. Then assumptive close.
4. **"I've been burned before / I've already paid $5k for the same offer."** — she went into defense mode. Right move: validate ("that's totally frustrating") → personal story / agency-vs-contractor parallel → emphasize what's *actually* different (30-mile radius, qualified booking, Facebook-only model) → assumptive close.
5. **"I need to think about it / send me an email."** — root cause: no pain established earlier. Fix #1 fixes this.

### Other patterns to clean up

- **"Let me talk to my partners" — kills authority.** She's the business owner on the call; she IS the decision maker. Saying she'll consult partners hands the frame back.
- **No probing on negative adjectives.** When prospect says "not enough jobs" she moves on. Should ask "enough?" and let them expand.
- **Pitching wrong things.** Nolan flagged she sometimes pitches Google ads or SEO — we don't do those. Pitch is Facebook-only, qualified appointment booking. Has to be tight or prospects say "someone offered me the same thing."

## Coaching priorities (ranked by revenue impact)

1. **Install the discovery flow.** Current → friction → impact → tried → why not double down → pain → pitch. Until this is in place every other fix is downstream.
2. **Drop "trust us / 5 years experience" lines entirely.** Replace with PGIHQ + math.
3. **Probe on negatives.** "Not enough?" "Bad?" "Frustrating?" — one-word probes.
4. **Stop saying "let me talk to my partners."** She's the owner.
5. **Tighten the pitch to the actual offer** — Facebook ads, qualified appointment booking, 30-mile radius, $1k setup + $44/day + $150/booked. Nothing else.
6. **First-appointment-free reframe** for risk objections (Aly's idea, now playbook).
