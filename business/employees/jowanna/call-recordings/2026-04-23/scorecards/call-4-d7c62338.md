## Key moments

- **Prospect counter-proposed (line 125-131):** *"I don't want to pay ad fee, ad spend. I want just flat contract commission. Boom. Done. $100,000 project, you get $2,500."* → Strong, specific objection. Required reframe using Matt Ryder's logical certainty: our per-appointment model actually costs you LESS than 2-2.5% if we hit our target close rate, and you keep control of margin. She didn't make that case.
- **Authority-killing line (line 158-159):** *"I can go back to my partners and talk to them about this."* → This is the #1 framework violation of the day. Script says SHE is the business owner. Once the prospect knows she's not the decision-maker, every closing moment evaporates.
- **Prospect called her out (line 191):** Something about "I actually am a sales agent" in the transcript — whether that's Jowanna saying it or the prospect saying it TO her, it means the authority frame was broken.

## Single biggest leverage point

**Stop offering to "go to your partners."** She is the owner on the call. If a prospect proposes a different deal, her script-aligned response is: *"I hear you — most of our best clients have the same instinct. Here's why the per-appointment model actually protects your margin better than percentage..."* and reframe. If she genuinely needs to check with partners, she does it OFF the call and reframes as "let me run your specific numbers and come back" — never "let me talk to my partners" in front of the prospect.
