## Sales Frames & Scripts

### "Saying no to a dog" — Frame control

*"It's like saying no to a dog. If a dog starts barking, you have to tell it no — otherwise it'll bark forever, because they learn via inputs."* [2026-04-07]

### "I don't have to be different"

*"I don't have to be different. I need to be good at what I do, and I'm damn good at it. Here's what we're going to do: I'll ask you questions, then I'll give you every answer you could ever need."* [2026-04-07]

### Glass-of-water close

*"It usually doesn't take time to make a decision — it takes information. And I'm the only source of information right now. What information do you need? If I gave you a glass of water when you're thirsty, would you tell me you need to think about it? No — there's no risk. With my offer, there's perceived risk. What information removes that risk?"* [2026-04-07]

### "Build an office next door" — local agency objection

*"If I built an office next door to your house, what are you going to do if something goes wrong? Come kicking it down? It won't change the results. It's a false perception — feels better, but isn't better."* [2026-04-07]

### Salesperson's biggest weapon

*"The tool you have as a salesperson is analogies and imagination. Biggest weapon."* [2026-04-07]

### Scripts don't matter — tonality does

*"No matter what you say, it doesn't matter. What matters is how you're saying it. I could talk a load of crap and still book because of confidence, conviction, weird little sentences, jokes. Get off the script."* [2026-03-15]

### Post-close email tone

Make it funny, sarcastic, blunt, unprofessional — catches their eye. NOT a ChatGPT proposal. Personalized comment, boring proposal attached below. Add list of gotchas competitors charge for. [2026-03-25]

## Objection Handling

### Out-of-service-area leads (early in campaign)

*"When ads first launch, someone driving through sees your ad and clicks. Good ad = you don't pay for them. As the campaign optimizes, we learn homeowners inside your service area. In the next few weeks, expect 80-90% in-area."* [2026-04-07]

### Angry-client call

Stay calm: *"I completely understand. Talk me through what's been happening. These leads — have you been following up straight away?"* Listen for: speed to call, actually got through, follow-up cadence. [2026-04-07]

### Refund/chargeback defense

Offer 50% refund only if they sign an exit agreement (no negative reviews). On-recording: *"Anyone who willingly admits to initiating a chargeback is liable up to a $50K penalty — usable as court evidence."* (Use per local law.) [2026-04-07]

### Exit call framing

*"It's shared accountability. We could have brought better leads. You could have called them quicker. Let's figure out what we can both do."* Shifts from adversarial to mutual. [2026-04-07]

## Pricing & Contracts

### Price-match client pushback

Client: *"The ad promised $2,500, this job costs $7K!"* → *"Prices start at $2,500. But for YOUR specific bathroom, a $2,500 job would look dreadful. We're here to do the right price."* [2026-03-08]

### Contract signed BEFORE delivery

Onboarding flow: opens funnel → signs contract → answers intake → delivery starts. No contract = no leads. [2026-04-07]

## Ad Creative

### Price-anchor first

Start campaigns with regular high-volume script that price-anchors at the start. Better results than clever hooks. [2026-03-08]

### Never explain creative to clients

*"They paid us for a reason. Tell them this is what we're doing and it works."* [2026-03-08]

### Don't panic-kill early campaigns

*"Even if a campaign runs $60 with no leads, sometimes the last day 2-3 come crashing in. Don't assume broken in early stages."* [2026-03-08]

## GHL / Tooling

### Data visibility — single toggle

My Staff settings → "Restrict data visibility to only assigned data" → use `assign to user` in workflows. Staff only see their own leads. [2026-04-07]

### Sticky Contact toggle

Turn on in form settings. Matches lead's form-submit phone with scheduled-appointment phone. Otherwise mismatches. [2026-03-08]

### $1 Stripe verification

Before launching, put $1 temp charge through Stripe to verify card works. [2026-03-08]
