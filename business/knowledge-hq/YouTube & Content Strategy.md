# YouTube & Content Strategy

<aside>
⚡ YouTube is the god of compound interest marketing. CAC = basically $0. Close rate from inbound = 50-70%.

</aside>

## Why YouTube

- Every other method has fluctuating costs. YouTube CAC is basically $0.
- Close rate from YouTube inbound: 50-70% vs 20-40% from cold outbound.
- Humans need ~7 hours of content to know, like, trust, and buy. Every 20-min video adds to that clock.

## Content Rules

- 20 minutes to record. 10 minutes to post. Every day.
- One-Take Tony. Do NOT edit. Stutter? Laugh and keep talking. It builds trust.
- 80% educational Miro board content. 20% lifestyle/social proof.

## Topics That Work

- 'Get 15 more appointments without ever working with a marketing agency again'
- 'I asked 3 landscapers their biggest marketing problem - here is what they said'
- 'Stop sharing leads with your competition'

## Timeline

- Month 1: Nothing much. Just post.
- Month 2: 1-2 inbound calls start appearing.
- Month 3+: Algorithm kicks in. 3+ calls/week from organic alone.
