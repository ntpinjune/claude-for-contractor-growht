# 🗺️ Niche Intelligence

## Niche Tier Rankings (Home Services)

**S-Tier:**

- Garage door replacement, Basement remodeling, Deck builders, Water restoration, Fire damage restoration, HVAC

**A-Tier:**

- Concrete & stamped concrete, Hardwood flooring, Roofing (saturated but viable), Mold remediation, Home additions, Electrical, Plumbing

**B-Tier:**

- Siding, Windows & doors, Fencing, Outdoor kitchens, Insulation (spray foam best)

**C-Tier:**

- Gutters, Painting, Flooring epoxy, Pressure washing, Roof cleaning

**D/E-Tier:**

- Bathroom remodeling (heavily saturated, low-barrier, lower quality contractors), Retaining walls, Foundation repair, Drywall

**F-Tier (AVOID):**

- **Landscaping** — heavily abused, low ticket, seasonal, low-IQ owners, poor renewals, terrible service delivery
- Land clearing, Site work/excavation, Demolition, Tree service, General web design

## Niche Selection Criteria (5 Factors)

1. **TAM:** 10K–1M potential clients (under 10K = too small; over 1M = oversaturated)
2. **Ticket size:** Higher AOV = fewer leads needed, lower CAC, higher client LTV
3. **Sophistication:** Sweet spot = medium (owners value marketing, not yet destroyed by competitors)
4. **Demand:** Needs > Wants (needs aren't affected by economy)
5. **Seasonality:** Year-round > seasonal

## Micro-Niche Strategy

- Sub-niche → sub-niche → micro-niche: Remodeling → Kitchen → Outdoor Kitchen
- Less saturation, easier to win, simpler to call out your ICP in ads
- Generate 10+ micro-niche ideas per broad niche using Claude AI
- Best performing: Pergola/shade structures, outdoor kitchens, pool deck installation, wrap-around porch builders

## Ads vs. SMS Economics (Real Numbers)

- Metric  |  Cold SMS  |  Paid Ads
- Monthly spend  |  $1,450  |  $1,500
- New clients/month  |  6  |  —
- AOV  |  $500-750  |  $2,400
- Chargeback rate  |  20%  |  ~1%
- Net profit  |  $2,000  |  $7,500
- Hours/day  |  10  |  5
- Effective hourly rate  |  **$9/hour**  |  **$60/hour**

SMS feels free. It isn't. It selects for broke, skeptical, high-dispute clients. Use SMS only if you have <$35K in bank and need immediate cash flow. Paid ads are the business.
