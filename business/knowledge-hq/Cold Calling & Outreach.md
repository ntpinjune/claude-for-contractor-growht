# Cold Calling & Outreach

## KPIs

- 100 dials = ~1 booked appointment (industry standard)
- Target for setters: 1-2 bookings per day minimum
- CAC under $100 = perfect. Under $300 = acceptable. Over $300 = diagnose.

## Best Times to Call

- 8:00-9:00 AM - Good
- 1:00 PM - Good window
- 3:00 PM onwards - Money time
- 7:00 PM Eastern = HIGHEST pick-up rate. People are home, comfortable.

## Follow-Up Cadence

- Triple-dial new leads. 3 times/day for 7 days straight.
- After 7 days: Dial Pot - call through old leads daily in free time.
- Text between calls: 'Hey sorry I missed you, just wanted to connect before our call.'

## Hiring Filipino Setters

- Run Facebook ads to Philippines. Fast and cheap. Jack was launching 3 campaigns in one week.
- Interview: Can they speak clearly? Do they command authority? Do you WANT to listen to them? If no to any - reject.
- Pay: $75 per client retained more than 24 hours (protects from kickbacks).

## SMS

- SMS is getting harder. Jack says basically bury SMS for landscapers - market is too blasted.
- ABR target: 50%+. Show rate target: 50%+. Below that consistently - move to cold calling or ads.

## Lead Scraping

- Use Outscraper (Google Maps scraper). Search niche + location. Pull leads. Feed to setters.

- After 7 days: move to "Dial Pot" — work through old leads in free time.

- Chasing/Hunting: Use backup phone number after 3 days — catch them off-guard.

## Hiring Filipino Setters

- Run Facebook ads to Philippines. Fast and cheap.
- Interview gate: Can they speak clearly? Command authority? Do you WANT to listen to them? Any no = reject.
- 3 staged interviews, each with a task to study. Contract + training before they touch leads.

## SMS System

### SMS Mindset

- SMS is getting harder for landscapers specifically — market too blasted. Focus on ads.
- Cold SMS economics: ~$9/hour effective wage vs. paid ads ~$60/hour. Not a close comparison.

### SMS Compliance Rules (Non-Negotiable)

- Max **800 SMS per sub-account per day**
- Per phone number: 200 (day 1, new number), 300-500 (warmed number with replies)
- **Turn OFF all reply-stop messages** (every compliance language setting off)
- **Never send to Texas** — illegal, account suspension risk
- Monitor monthly: Optout rate ≤1%; delivery rate ≥92%; error 30007 = carriers blocking you (very dangerous)
- No swearing, no hashtags, no scary emojis, no images — plain text only
- Casual tone, occasional grammar mistakes = normal for contractors

### SMS Strategy

- **Bait approach** (outperforms transparent): *"I've got 5 guys needing full roofing jobs this week"* — implies subcontractor work
- Attracts serious operators (not desperate prospects looking for a handout)
- Test 3 different messages per sub-account simultaneously
- Minimum test: 500+ SMS/message/day for 6 days (3,000 SMS per script before deciding)
- Scripts burn out — always testing is survival

### Area Code Strategy

- Match local area code to your lead list's dominant area code (ask ChatGPT to analyze list)
- OR use high-authority states: Wyoming, New York (businesses recognize and answer)
- Avoid Miami 786 — associated with scams, terrible answer rate
- Maintain a backlog of phone numbers; delete blocked/spam-flagged numbers immediately

### SMS Metrics — 10-10-50-20 Framework

- 10% positive reply rate → 10% booking rate (from replies) → 50% show rate → 50% close rate
- Example: 1,000 SMS → 100 replies → 10 booked → 5 shows → 1-2 closes
- **"Positive reply" is NOT "yes, 100% interested"** — it's "Who are you?" / "What's this?" / "Where are you located?" Curiosity = highest quality reply.

### Show-Up Mind Manipulation (Nickname Gambit)

1. **Setting call opener:** *"I texted you about some quotes — yeah? Good. What's your name? [David.] David, I have work and need to outsource to a couple contractors. Already working with 3 guys in your area. Wanted to see if we'd be good partners — sound good?"*
2. **The pitch:** *"I can book out your diary for the next 2 months. Takes about a week. This isn't free — costs us both money. We both have skin in the game. If you're serious about growing, you're the one I want to work with."*
3. **The nickname gambit:** *"Save me as 'Jack with Jobs' right now — can you do that?"* Wait while they do it.
4. **Confirmation:** *"When you see your phone ring tonight at 4 p.m., make sure you answer for 'Jack with Jobs.' Do that for me?"*
5. **Why it works:** People use nicknames for friends/trusted people. Brain sees "Jack with Jobs" = warm emotion = they answer and arrive warmer.
6. **NO pre-call social proof** — don't send VSL/testimonials before the call. It kills curiosity. Show the VSL ON the call (auto-play on Google Meet), then testimonials, then pitch.

### Lead Scraping

- Use Outscraper (Google Maps): search niche + location, pull Name + Phone only
- Verify with ClearOut: filter for Mobile only, delete landline/VoIP/toll-free
- Upload to GHL smart list. Add filter: "Tag is empty" so each lead gets SMS only once.
