## The Problem Cycle (Stages by Revenue)

- **0-10K/month:** You're the sole operator. Learn everything yourself.
- **10-20K/month:** Get VAs, hire CSMs. Don't skip this.
- **20-50K/month:** Main problem = CHURN. Standardize fulfillment or die. Every onboarding must be automated or easily delegable.
- **50-140K/month:** You're the bottleneck. Hire closers. Hire an Ops Wizard (COO).
- **140-250K/month:** Pod structure. Mini-teams (acquisition, delivery, client success).
- **Foundation over ego:** Running 80-90% margins means you're not scaling. Invest in team and systems, take lower personal margin, grow faster.
