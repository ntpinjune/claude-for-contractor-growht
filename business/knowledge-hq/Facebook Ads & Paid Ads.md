# Facebook Ads & Paid Ads

## Lead Quality Levers (in order)

- 1. Soft disqualifiers on landing page / lead form description - try first
- 2. Soft qualifier callout IN the ad creative
- 3. Hard qualifiers in lead form questions - last resort, kills volume

## Key Metrics

- CTR low = creative not resonating. Change the creative.
- CPC high + CTR low = creative problem, not audience problem.
- CPM high = audience too small or competitive. Broaden.
- Show rate target: 50%+

## Ad Creative Rules

- Test 4 similar image ads simultaneously. Let data pick the winner.
- Turn off ads with terrible CTR immediately.
- Price hook: 'You should not pay more than $1,000 for a vinyl window' - true, specific, competitive.
- Video ads: CapCut. 20 minutes. Done.

## Campaign Structure

- Keep winning ads running. Duplicate and test variations alongside.
- Do not launch on holidays.

## Lead Quality Levers (in order)

1. Soft disqualifiers on landing page / lead form description
2. Soft qualifier callout IN the ad creative
3. Hard qualifiers in lead form (last resort — kills volume)

## Key Metrics

- CTR (link click) low (<1.5%) = creative not engaging. Change the hook.
- CPC high + CTR low = creative problem, not audience.
- CPM high ($60+) = audience too small or competitive. Broaden radius.
- Frequency >2.5 + rising CPL = creative fatigue. Swap hook.
- Show rate target: 50%+

## CPL Benchmarks by Niche

- HVAC: under $10 = good
- Roofing: ~$7 winning; $30+ underperforming
- Concrete: $10-$30 range
- Epoxy: $30 acceptable if AOV $5-$20K
- Landscaping/deck contractors: varies by market size

## Campaign Ops Rules

- Keep winning ads running. Duplicate and test variations alongside — never edit the live winner.
- Do not launch on holidays.
- Never change two things at once. One variable per test.
- Increasing budget >20% on a live ad set resets the learning phase.
- Meta CPM rule: Use odd budget numbers ($95 beats $100 — Meta clusters round-number advertisers).

## Engagement Campaigns (Page Building)

- Run before or alongside client campaign
- Objective: Engagement (not leads/sales)
- Creative: Dog picture + "Follow my page" copy
- Budget: $25/day
- Audience: US or specific region
- Multi-advertiser: OFF
