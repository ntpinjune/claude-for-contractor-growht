# 🚀 Campaign Delivery System

## Core Principle: Own the Assets

- **NEVER use a client's ad account.** Own the page, pixel, landing page, ad account.
- Build business asset value (can sell business for 6-7x annual profit vs. 2x without assets)
- Amex Gold Business for ad spend: 4x points on purchases

## Unbranded Brand Pages

- Create broad reusable pages: "Home Service Pros USA", "Home Improvement Contractors USA"
- One Facebook/Instagram page per niche
- Build following with engagement ads (dog pictures: "Give me a follow" — $25/day, runs before client launch)
- Bio: SEO-optimized, emojis, state-wide service area, all relevant services listed

## Campaign Naming Convention

**Format: `AS55_$44/day_CreativeTest_March11`**

- AS = your initials (ad setter)
- 55 = client code number
- $44/day = daily budget
- CreativeTest = test type (CreativeTest / PriceTest / AudienceTest / VideoTest)
- March11 = launch date

## Audience Settings (Exact)

- Age: **27-65** (under 27 can't afford home services)
- All genders, all languages
- Placements: **Facebook Feed + Instagram Feed ONLY**
- Turn OFF: Stories, Messenger, Reels, In-stream, Audience Network
- Targeting: Trust the algorithm — do NOT over-restrict with detailed targeting
- Advantage+ targeting: ON for landing page campaigns
- Multi-advertiser ads: **OFF** (most commonly missed setting — prevents your ad appearing beside competitors)

## Ad Budget Rules

- Daily budget for testing: $44-100/day odd numbers (e.g., **$95 beats $100** — Meta clusters round-number advertisers together in worse audiences)
- Minimum per ad set: $20/day for learning
- **Never increase budget >20%** in a single day on a live ad set — resets the learning phase
- B2B data window: $100/day = 1.5-day window. $100/day is the sweet spot.
- Ad set structure: Split budget equally (e.g., $44/day total → $22 A1 Video, $22 A2 Image)

## Campaign Structure

- One campaign per niche/offer
- One ad set initially with **6-8 creatives** (Andromeda algorithm rewards high creative variety)
- Later: Winner ad set (most budget) + Tester ad set
- CBO preferred for landing page campaigns; ABO for lead campaigns

## 8-Point Pre-Launch Checklist

1. **Price matches script** — video says $12,500, copy must say $12,500 exactly
2. **Captions visible** — play on phone, captions sit mid-screen (not cut off at bottom)
3. **Numbers not words** — show "$5,500" not "five thousand five hundred" (word conversion = friction)
4. **Correct aspect ratio** — images 1:1, feed videos 1.9:6 (vertical), stories 4:5/9:16
5. **Multi-advertiser OFF** — check in ad settings, most commonly missed
6. **CTA = "Learn More"** — NEVER "Book Now", "Sign Up", or "Get Quote" (learn more = lowest friction)
7. **Correct pixel + correct event** — confirm Lead event (not PageView) using Meta Pixel Helper
8. **Landing page works on mobile** — click it yourself, submit test form, verify thank you page

## Ad Creatives

### Lean Mean Machine Script (Exact Template)

> *"A brand new [SERVICE] for less than $[PSYCH NUMBER] in [CITY/STATE]. We're locally owned and operated — not a massive company with 20 offices, 50 trucks, and billboards everywhere. We run a lean, efficient operation: a small expert team, our own trusted crews, smart systems, and tight project management. That means lower overhead for us and better pricing for you. We use the same high-quality materials as the big-name [SERVICE] companies — without charging two or three times more. Click the Learn More button below to get your free, no-pressure estimate."*
> 

**Price psychology rules:**

- NEVER round numbers: not $7,000, not $10,000
- Use psychological prices: $7,495, $9,700, $10,500, $12,995
- "For less than $X" = volume (more leads, lower quality)
- "Starting at $X" = quality (fewer leads, higher conversion)

### Quad Image Format (High Performer)

- 4-quadrant collage: top-left job/work, top-right family with sign, bottom-left job/work, bottom-right family with sign
- 1:1 square, high resolution, authentic (not stock)

### Hook Categories (What Stops the Scroll)

1. **Chaos** — woman dropping groceries, man about to trip, dog bolting out
2. **Attractive person** — walks into frame or turns to camera in first second
3. **Reveal** — before/after within 2 seconds
4. **Job in progress** — time-lapse or active work footage
- First 2 seconds = hook ONLY. Never branding, logo, or company name first.

### AI Hook Generation (Runway ML Video 3)

- Use Video 3 for HOOKS only (not full ads)
- Generate 20-50 hooks per session (open 5-10 tabs simultaneously)
- Framework: 1 body + 1 CTA + test 10-50 hook variations
- Find winning hook → duplicate body → test new hooks alongside → repeat
- Andromeda rewards variation aggressively

### 70/20/10 Creative Testing Rule

- 70% of ads: Model proven winners (competitor ads running 30+ days = winners)
- 20% of ads: Variations on winning themes
- 10% of ads: Wild cards (often become best performer)

### Secret Shopping Competitors

- Facebook Ads Library → search "[niche] leads" or "[niche] contractor leads"
- Filter ads running 30+ days (no one runs losing ads long-term)
- Extract: hook structure, offer framing, pain points, CTA — then recreate in your own words
- Physically secret shop: Pose as prospect, go through their funnel, book their call, extract their scripts and pricing

## Decision Rules (Spend-Based)

- Spend  |  Metrics  |  Action
- <$30  |  Any  |  Do NOT touch anything
- $30-90  |  Good metrics, no leads  |  WAIT — leads arrive in clusters
- $30-90  |  Bad metrics (CTR <1%, CPM $60+)  |  Iterate creative (hook swap)
- >$90  |  Good metrics, no leads  |  Backend problem — check pixel/landing page
- >$90  |  Bad metrics + no leads  |  Kill and iterate

**The 90-Rule:** Don't make creative decisions until $90+ spent. Data doesn't lie — but only when there's enough of it.

## Iteration Ladder (Creative Underperforming)

1. **Price change** — same visual, same script, different price point
2. **Hook swap** — change first 2 seconds, keep body identical
3. **Body refresh** — keep hook, change middle section
4. **Full new ad** — if levels 1-3 exhausted

When iterating: duplicate the ad set (don't edit live), make ONE change, launch the duplicate at midnight, keep original running, compare CPLs after 5-7 days. Never edit a live winning ad.

## When to Kill an Ad (ALL conditions must be met)

- >$90 spent AND zero leads AND CTR <1% AND CPM >$60 AND frequency >2.5

## Scaling Winners

- **Horizontal scaling > vertical scaling** (more ad sets > bigger budget on one)
- Vertical: Never >20% budget increase per day (resets learning phase)
- Horizontal: Duplicate winning ad set → change body (keep hook) → launch alongside → run 5-7 days → kill loser

## Testing Phase vs. Scaling Phase

- **Testing (3-5 days):** Use lead forms — fast volume, find winning creative
- **Scaling:** Switch to landing pages — fewer leads, higher-quality appointments
- Don't run landing pages with untested creatives. Test first, then scale with confidence.

## Landing Page Setup

- White background (fastest load)
- Logo + risk-reversal headline + sub-headline + booking calendar
- Light testimonials only (no video on landing page — video distracts and slows load)
- Mobile-tested for speed and form functionality
- Soft disqualifier at top: "Do not book if you are: [conditions]"

## Lead Form Hidden Disqualification

- Question 1: Business ownership (Yes/No; No → close form)
- Question 2: Monthly revenue ($0-8K → close form silently; $8K+ → advance)
- **Do NOT tell prospects they're rejected** — they'll lie on the form
- Phone number field dragged to top, labeled "Check your mobile number is correct"

## Onboarding (30-Minute Call)

- **0-5 min:** Show similar client results, set realistic timeline, explain best/worst/average case
- **5-20 min:** Walk client through GHL live — call the lead, text the lead, download Lead Connector app
- **20-30 min:** Collect credit card ($1 Stripe charge minimum), send launch confirmation workflow, client signs onboarding form
- Go live same day. Promise 7 days; deliver in 2 days or same night.
- *"Those leads weren't from ads yet — they're from SEO. Ads just went live today."* (Positions you as superhuman even when true.)

## Client Communication & Retention

- **68% of clients cancel because they felt ignored — not because of bad results** (14% cancel due to actual results)
- Week 1: Daily calls minimum
- Week 2: Every other day
- Week 3+: Weekly calls + texts every couple days
- Send Loom videos showing you working. Show screenshots of campaign activity. Perceived effort > actual effort.
- When leads slow: Show them new work (website built, video ad created, new landing page) — keep their mind busy

## Reset Call Script (Verbatim)

> *"Listen, you're obviously not happy. I see you texting every single day with complaints, and I'm going to make sure that stops happening — because I have a service that's working for all my clients. But it's not working for you. So I need to figure out why. What's different about your campaign? Let's spend the next 10 minutes going through everything: lead quality, volume we've given you. Then I'll give my side of the story and we can figure out what to do from here."*
> 

Come prepared with: lead count delivered, appointments booked, client's own conversion rate. If they converted 2 of 20 leads, the problem is their sales process, not your ads.

## CPL Factors (What Drives Cost Per Lead Up/Down)

1. Market size (under 300K population = naturally higher CPL)
2. Service type (higher AOV = acceptable higher CPL)
3. Campaign age (fresh = higher; 2+ weeks = lower)
4. Ad creative quality (low CTR = creative problem)
5. Landing page friction (more questions = higher CPL)
6. Pixel data (niche pixel with 1,000s of leads = much better optimization)
7. Audience fatigue (frequency >2.5 + rising CPL = swap hook)
8. Seasonality

## No-Leads Troubleshooting (10-Step)

1. No impressions at all → audience too small, budget too low, ad rejected
2. Good CTR + no leads → NOT a creative problem; check landing page and pixel
3. Bad CTR (<1%) → creative not engaging; hook swap
4. High CPM (>$60) → market saturation; expand radius or improve creative
5. Good metrics, zero leads <$90 → WAIT. Leads come in clusters.
6. Good metrics, zero leads >$90 → landing page or pixel issue
7. High CTR, low form conversions → form friction; reduce to 1-2 questions
8. Pixel not firing → use Meta Pixel Helper; verify Lead event fires on form submit
9. Everything looks right → check domain is live and resolving
10. Small population area → $70-100 CPL is normal; viable if AOV covers it
