# Objection Handling

<aside>
⚡ Pre-handle BEFORE they come up. The best objection handle happens before the objection is raised.

</aside>

## Think About It

- PRE-HANDLE: 'You have probably dealt with tire kickers who say think about it, sleep on it, talk to their wife, their dog, their wall. Can you do me a favor and not do that?'
- If it comes up: 'What exactly do you need to think about?' Isolate the real objection.
- 'From business owner to business owner, it is usually either trust or money - which one is it for you?'

## Need to Talk to Business Partner

- Pre-handle at start: 'Is everyone who needs to make a decision on this call today?'
- If comes up: 'I have a partner too. We used to ask each other to buy a stapler. Does he trust you to make a decision here?'
- 'What happens if he says no? Are you going to let him be the reason you do not even try?' Works 50% of time. Other 50%: take a deposit.

## Too Expensive

- Isolate: 'Is it that you will be flat broke, or just uncomfortable paying upfront?' Usually uncomfortable not broke.
- If genuinely tight: reduce ad spend NOT your fee. 'Instead of $44/day we do $25/day.'

## No Trust

- Establish trust BEFORE pitch: 'It is useless to pitch you anything unless you believe I can help you.' Show story + proof. 'Do you believe I am good at what I do?' Get YES then continue.
- 'We work with over 1,000 clients. One bad review out of 1,000 - is that worth worrying about?'

## Contract Objections

- No guarantee: 'How could I guarantee an ROI from all the way over here? If you were me, how would you do that?' Logic kills it.
- General: Use LOGIC. Walk them into reality. 'Who benefits if this goes wrong? I have 73 employees and 200 clients - is $1K worth a bad review to me?'

## Timing Objections

- 'It takes us one week to set up. If we start today, everything is ready when you are.'
- 'How much longer do you want to stay at your current revenue?'

> Pre-handle BEFORE they come up. The best objection handle happens before the objection is raised.
> 

> Universal framework: **Disarm → Isolate → Clarify the real objection → Handle with logic/sarcasm/tonality → Rule of 3 (max 3 attempts then walk away)**
> 

## Think About It

- PRE-HANDLE: *"You've probably dealt with tire kickers who say think about it, sleep on it, talk to their wife, their dog, their wall. Can you do me a favor and not do that at the end of this call?"*
- If it surfaces — the logic argument:
- *"A lot of people tell me they want to think about it. And you know what happens? They never come back and nothing changes."*
- *"It doesn't take time to make decisions — it takes information. And I'm your only source of information right now. What's going to change?"*
- *"Whether we look at this for 15 minutes, 15 days, or 15 years — it still looks exactly the same. What is it that's really stopping you?"*

## Money / Budget Objection

1. Disarm: *"Don't worry about it, I've got you."*
2. Isolate: *"Apart from the money — are you happy with everything else?"*
3. Clarify the REAL objection: *"Is it that you physically don't have the money in your account, or are you concerned about spending it and not getting a return?"*
- "Don't have the money" = different handle than "worried about results"
1. Zero risk reframe: *"Both decisions are risk-free. But only one of them will help you achieve your goal. Which risk-free decision gets you there?"*
2. Not trying IS the risk: *"The riskiest thing you can do is not try. Every business owner knows that."*
3. If they're truly tight: reduce ad spend, NOT your fee. *"Instead of $44/day we do $25/day."*

## Need to Talk to Partner / Spouse

- PRE-HANDLE at call start: *"Is everyone who needs to make a decision on this call today?"*
- If it surfaces:
1. Acknowledge with humor: *"What's your partner's name? Oh, it's Lisa — so Lisa is the reason we're not moving forward. Got you."*
2. Isolate: *"Apart from Lisa, is there anything else that would stop you?"*
3. Build awareness: *"Is she aware of the problems you've had with lead generation? Does she know you're talking to me now?"*
4. Probe authority: *"You're not really asking permission — you're just running it by her, right?"*
5. Reframe: *"You can't let someone who equally needs this to work be the reason you never even try. What's the smartest decision for both of you?"*

## Too Expensive / No Trust

- Isolate: *"Is it that you'll be flat broke, or just uncomfortable paying upfront?"* Usually uncomfortable, not broke.
- No trust handle: *"Would there be any reason why it would be in our best interest to risk our reputation for $1,300? We need you paying us every month for 15 years — that's money. $1,300 keeps the lights on."* (Make the amount sound trivially small.)
- For scam fears: Show testimonials page, offer addresses, use logic: *"One bad review out of 1,000 — is that worth worrying about?"*

## No Card / I'm Driving

- Driving: *"Can I ask a cheeky question — do you have brakes on your car? [laugh] I'm joking. Can you pull over for a second so we just get you started?"* They almost always pull over.
- No card: *"Really? David, if you don't want to sign up, you can just tell me. [laugh] Do you have Apple Pay or Google Pay? Can I text you the payment link right now?"*

## Competitors / Need to Shop Around

- Consolidate first: Confirm price is fine, the service is fine, they believe it would work. Get those yeses.
- Then: *"Usually when people find the gold mine and all the boxes are ticked, they get out the shovel and start digging — they don't go looking for other coal mines. Are we the gold mine? Do we have everything you need?"*
- Create urgency: *"Starting today saves you $1,000. What would you spend that $1,000 on?"*

## "You're the Same as Competitors"

- *"What do we do that's the same? Oh, you run Facebook ads? What about the 16 platforms, the AI targeting that only puts you in front of likely buyers, the full 5-step qualification, and the AI that follows up and books appointments? You've had all that before? I don't believe you."*

## Rule of Three

- Handle an objection max 3 times. After 3: *"It's been a pleasure speaking with you. I'm not going to push anymore. If you have any questions, reach out."* Push the sale away — if they pull it back, re-engage. If not, they're cold.
