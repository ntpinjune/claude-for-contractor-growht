# Sales & Closing

## The Sales Call Structure

- STEP 1 - Pitch yourself first: Show story, experience, social proof BEFORE the pitch. 'It does not matter what I am about to pitch unless you believe in me as a human being.'
- STEP 2 - Discovery: Find what they tried, where they are, where they want to be. Every answer is a future objection handle or pitch ammunition.
- STEP 3 - Pre-handle objections BEFORE pitching: 'You have probably dealt with tire kickers. Can you do me a favor and not think about it at the end of this call?'
- STEP 4 - Pitch: (1) Proof/credibility (2) Service overview (3) Adaptive Pitching using their pain points (4) Unique Mechanism (5) Exciting next steps
- STEP 5 - Future pace BEFORE price drop. Then future pace AGAIN after. Double reinforcement.
- STEP 6 - One-liner close. Simple. Direct.

## Tonality

- Be arrogant, cocky, above them. You should NOT want to be on the phone with them MORE than they don't want to be on the phone with you.
- Be snappy and quick. There is fire in you. They feel it.
- Other telemarketers say 'Hey how is your day going!' - do the OPPOSITE.
- Never send a Loom video saying how excited you are to see them. You are sealing the coffin.

## Speed to Lead

- Intent is highest the MOMENT they click the ad. Call immediately after form submission.
- Call them, do discovery on the phone, then: 'Do you have 5 minutes to jump on a quick Google Meet?' Stay on the phone until they join. Close on Meet.
- Booked call in 1 hour? Call them NOW. 90% will say yes to pulling it forward.

## ROI Close

- 'If you convert 70% of estimates to jobs, and I bring people who want quotes - 70% of what you pay closes. Would you pay $150 to get $15,000 out? That is a 10X ROI.'

## After Close

- 'After this call you will get a welcome email. I need signature + first week ad spend. If you can do that now - delays us otherwise.'
- Get contract signed BEFORE launching ads. Always.

## Pipeline Stages (CRM)

- **Fresh 72** → leads in first 72 hours; call immediately
- **Loose** → leads past 72 hours; grind these hardest
- **Confirm Now** → booked, triARcript done, social proof auto-sent
- **Chasing** → missed follow-up; call 2-3x/day
- **Hunting** → 3 days in Chasing with no contact; auto-moved
- **Unresponsive** → call less frequently
- **Lost** → final stage

## Loose Leads Call Script

- Opener with tonality: *"Dave, do you still need more line clearing jobs for the business or do you have all the jobs you can handle right now?"*
- ROI math: *"How many jobs do you think you'd get from 100 estimates? And how much per job? So that's $X in 90 days — or we work free of charge."*
- Create scarcity: *"Sounds too good to be true, right? Let me prove it on a quick video call — 20 minutes now or later today."*
- **CRITICAL**: If they want to call back, pin an exact time: *"What time later?"* — never let them leave without a commitment.

## Booked Call Prep (Before Every Call)

1. Run triARcript call to confirm they have the link and CRM is updated
2. Call 10 minutes before: *"Hopping on in 10 minutes — just making sure you've got the link."*
3. Ask them to save your number
4. Pre-call setup: script up, pitch deck ready, GHL contact open, good lighting, good audio, coffee/water

## Sales Call Structure (Full KYC System)

### Stage 1: KYC — This Is Where the Sale Is Made

**Opening (Authority + Rapport):**

- *"My name is [X]. I'm a Solution Expert here at [Agency]. A bit of background on me — I'm based in [location]. Whereabouts are you calling from? And is it your business we're talking about today?"*
- You are a Solution Expert, not a salesperson. Authority from second one.

**The North Star Question (do not accept surface answers):**

- *"What specifically made you decide to book the call with me today?"*
- If they say "just looking around" — push: *"Why are you looking NOW vs. six months ago? What's happening with the business?"*
- Dig until you find their real motivation. Nobody spends 30 minutes just to learn.

**Discovery: Past → Present → Now → Future**

**PAST** — History + pain:

- *"What happened? Why did it go wrong? How long ago?"* Let them tell the full story. Their past pain = your future objection handles and pitch ammunition.

**PRESENT** — Current state:

- *"How long has this been a problem? Big problem or just a minor one? What would you change if you could?"* Relax your tone here.

**NOW** — Current needs:

- *"What type of jobs do you want more of? Average job value? How far do you travel?"*

**FUTURE** — Gap + aspiration:

- First, make them acknowledge change is necessary: *"To grow the business as fast as possible, you'll have to make some change — right?"* If no → deal won't close.
- *"How many jobs do you want monthly? Can you handle them? What would you do at 30 — franchise?"* Walk them all the way to their vision.
- Create fear of inaction: *"What happens if you don't do anything? Does the business still grow on its own?"*

### Stage 2: Pre-Close Objection Handling

- Any objection that surfaces: *"Great — we'll cover all of that. But can I tell you about us and what happens from here, just for five minutes?"* Get the yes, then go into trust/pitch.

### Stage 3: The Pitch

- Open by recapping their pain: *"You told me [X]. Your main problem is [Y]."*
- Tailor EVERYTHING to what they said in discovery. If quality was bad: *"If we filtered leads so only serious buyers came through, you'd close more jobs — right?"* If volume was low: *"With 16 platforms and AI targeting, you telling me you won't have enough jobs?"*
- **Consolidation (temperature test):** *"How does that sound? Do you feel like that could help?"* — read if they're warm or cold before pricing.

### Stage 4: Close Sequence (4 Yeses → Assumption)

1. **Budget question:** *"Do you have a rough monthly budget in mind, or do you want me to advise on what most invest in your sector?"*
2. **Confirm budget fits:** *"Does that fit into the budget you had in mind?"*
3. **Temperature test / soft close:** *"Based on what we've discussed, do you feel like this system could help grow the business?"* → get YES
4. **Divot close (schedule):** *"When would you like to schedule your initial onboarding call to get things started?"* — don't ask IF, ask WHEN. *"Would Tuesday or Wednesday work better?"*
5. **Hard close (assumption):** *"Great — getting started today saves you $500. How would you like to pay that?"*

**THE RULE: Four yeses before you ask for money.** Yes on pain → yes on solution → yes on fit → yes on next step → THEN payment. Makes it very unlikely they say no.

**At close — LISTEN CLOSELY:** If they say *"I can do it now, just give me a minute"* — do NOT say "When can I call you back?" Send the payment link immediately and stay on the line. Missing this one cue can kill a 45-minute call.

## Tonality & Energy

- Be the higher-status person. You do not NEED them.
- Three tonalities: **concerned** (care), **curious** (genuine questions), **enthusiastic** (energy)
- Other telemarketers: "Hey how is your day going!" — do the OPPOSITE.
- Your state determines theirs. High energy → they mirror it. Low energy → deal dies.
- Jokes are powerful but timing matters: **No jokes in first 5 minutes.** Authority first. Humor at 10+ minutes in, when rapport is built.

## Speed to Lead

- Intent is highest the MOMENT they click the ad. Call immediately.
- Within 5 minutes = 21% higher conversion vs. 30+ minutes.
- Booked call in 1 hour? Call them NOW — 90% will pull it forward.
