# Pricing & Offers

## Best Offer Models

- 1. Pay-per-appointment (auto-charge) - BEST for scaling. Lowest friction.
- 2. Pay-per-lead - Easy delivery. High retention. Higher LTV.
- 3. Monthly retainer with upfront - Works but limits how fast you scale.

## Exact Numbers

- Pay-per-lead: $40-55 per lead. $45 is the sweet spot.
- Pay-per-appointment: $150-250 per shown appointment.
- Setup fee: $197-$350 to lower friction and fill pipeline fast.
- SDR compensation: $150-175 per close (fixed from setup fee - NOT commission %).

## CAC Benchmarks

- Under $100 CAC = perfect
- $100-300 CAC = acceptable
- Over $300 CAC = something is wrong

## Pricing Philosophy

- Lower price = clients stay longer = higher LTV. Churn increases the more you charge.
- If card declines: pause ads until payment. Do not negotiate.
- If client says 'only 5 leads were good': 'Every lead we deliver, you pay for.'

## Best Offer Models

1. **Pay-per-appointment (auto-charge)** — BEST for scaling. Lowest friction.
2. **Pay-per-lead** — Easy delivery. High retention. Higher LTV.
3. **Monthly retainer** — DO NOT USE. Very hard to sell. Creates dependency risk.

## Exact Numbers

- Pay-per-lead: $75-100/lead. Never call it a "lead" — call it an "estimate request."
- Script: *"You only pay us when a real homeowner in your area requests an estimate. No results, no invoice."*
- Pay-per-appointment (PPSA): 50% premium over PPL (e.g., PPL $100 → PPA $150)
- Setup fee: $197-$350 to lower friction and fill pipeline fast
- SDR compensation: $150-175/close or 10% recurring profit share (for VAs)

## PPSA Model (Pay Per Shown Appointment)

Three tiers:

1. **Paper Lead** → you generate the lead
2. **Paper Appointment** → you generate + book
3. **Paper Showed Appointment (PPSA)** → you generate, book, and ensure they show

PPSA has significantly higher LTV because value is linear with cost — retention stays high, churn drops dramatically vs. paper lead (50%+ churn on paper lead vs. much lower with PPSA).

**PPSA Pricing Formula (AOV-based):**

- Calculate cost to generate a shown appointment → mark up 2x
- $10K AOV niche: charge ~$150/shown appointment
- $20K AOV niche: charge ~$350/shown appointment
- $30K+ AOV niche: charge ~$450+/shown appointment

**Credit Policy (No Refunds):**

- Any booked appointment that didn't show AND was not rescheduled within 7 days = credit
- **No refunds — only store credits** (run through Force Charge / Lead Flow)
- End of month: count no-shows in pipeline → add that many credits → they auto-deplete over 3 billing cycles → then auto-charge resumes
- Charge at booking time (not show time). If client doesn't confirm show/no-show via email, they're billed regardless.

## CAC Benchmarks

- Under $100 CAC = perfect
- $100-300 CAC = acceptable
- Over $300 CAC = something is wrong

## Pricing Philosophy

- Lower price = longer retention = higher LTV. Churn increases the more you charge.
- If card declines: pause ads until payment. Do not negotiate.
- If client says "only 5 leads were good": *"Every lead we deliver, you pay for."*
