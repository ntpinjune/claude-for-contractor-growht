# 💼 PPSA Ad Creative System

## ICP Callout Formula (Critical)

- **Bad:** "Remodelers — our offer"
- **Good:** "Outdoor kitchen remodelers over $200K/year — listen up"
- Even better: "Outdoor kitchen remodelers with 2+ crews over $200K/year"
- They see themselves → think "Oh shit, this is me" → much higher CTR

## Psychological Reading Flow (Top to Bottom)

1. Primary text (read first): Callout + pain
2. Image: Contractor sitting with customer taking an estimate (end destination, not the product)
3. Headline: Final offer reinforcement
4. Description: Objection removal

## V1 Primary Text Formula (Pain-First)

1. **Contrary belief** — something that surprises them: *"You're doing over $200K/year building decks."*
2. **Rhythm/narrative** — plays in their head: *"One week you get more quotes than you can handle. The next week your phone goes quiet."*
3. **Show you understand** — *"You're chasing referrals."*
4. **Nudge** — *"Here's what we do differently."*
5. **The pitch** — *"We run targeted ads so that if they don't show, you simply don't pay."*
6. **CTA** — *"Click below. Claim your spots."*

## Offer Reinforcement (Say It 3+ Times)

- "Pay only for shown appointments" — in primary text
- Image shows contractor taking appointment (visual reinforcement)
- Headline: "Only pay per shown appointment"
- Description: "No ad spend. No contracts."
- Repetition creates intent → intent creates clicks → clicks create bookings → bookings create revenue
- **DO NOT put the price in the headline** (big number distracts, limited characters, looks cluttered)

## Micro Niche Framework

- **Niche → Sub-niche → Micro-niche**: Remodeling → Kitchen Remodeling → Outdoor Kitchen Remodeling
- TAM minimum: over 300,000 people in territory — block onboarding below this threshold
- Deck micro niches: Pergola/shade structures (Jack's top pick), pool deck, rooftop/elevated, wrap-around porch, screen porch enclosure, waterfront decking, bi-material/composite, outdoor kitchen deck, lighting/electrical specialists, restoration/refinishing
- Use Claude AI deep research to find sub-niches nobody else is targeting — if 50 agencies target same macro niche, results degrade

## ICP Definition

- Research dimensions: AOV, profit margins, common pain points (use Gemini Deep Research or Claude AI)
- Quality filter: English-speaking, serious project buyers
- Example call-out: "Outdoor Kitchen Remodelers Over $200K/Year"

## Image Selection Rule

- Show: Contractor sitting at table doing estimate/quote with customer (end destination)
- NOT: Happy family using the finished product
- Why: Visualizes the desired outcome (closing jobs), not just the mechanism
- Tool: Art List AI for generating images

## Campaign Phase System

- **Phase 1 (Days 1-5, Testing):** Lead forms only. Validate creative + offer. Fast feedback on CTR/CPL/winning angle.
- **Phase 2 (Scaling):** Switch winning creative to landing pages. Higher friction = higher intent = better showed appointment quality.
- Rule: Don't test on landing pages. Validate via lead forms first, then relaunch winner on landing page.

## Lead Form vs Landing Page

- **Lead Form:** Auto-fills contact info, low friction, lower intent — use for testing
- **Landing Page:** Manual entry, high friction, high intent — use for scaling
- **60/40 rule:** Applied to showed appointments (not leads) — 60% volume / 40% quality
- **Soft disqualifier script:** "Do not fill in if you are price shopping. Do not fill in if not looking to complete the project within six months."

## Quality Control Levers (in order of impact)

1. Offer quality (most important): "Only pay per show" attracts serious buyers; "50% off" attracts tire kickers
2. Creative quality: Low-quality creative attracts low-quality people
3. Price anchor: Advertised price must match real price
4. Friction/steps: More steps = higher intent applicant
5. Bait vs truth: Never say "pay per book" if you mean "pay per show"

## VA Setter: Full Booking Call Structure

1. Open
2. Confirm project type
3. Confirm location / service area
4. Confirm timeline — "next three months" acceptable; "next year" = do not book
5. Confirm budget
6. Confirm all decision makers available
7. Book appointment
8. Close + update CRM notes

**TCPA compliance:**

- B2B calls: Can record without disclosure
- B2C calls: Must state "This call is recorded for training and compliance purposes"
- Call hours: Never outside 8am–7pm local time

## PPSA Delivery Flow (Sky High View)

1. Run ads → lead forms (Phase 1 testing)
2. Determine winning creative/offer (3-5 days)
3. Leads → GHL CRM → VA calls + books appointment
4. VA confirms appointment before show
5. Client shows → client marks show/no-show via email
6. If no-show: VA hounds them to reschedule; no reschedule in 7 days → credit
7. Month-end: count no-shows → add credits in ForceCharge → deplete over 3 billing cycles → auto-charge resumes
8. Switch winning ad to landing page (Phase 2 scaling)

**Charge timing:** Card charged at booking (not at show). Email warns: "If you don't mark show/no-show, you're charged $200 regardless."
