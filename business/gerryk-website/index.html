Frequently Asked

Frequently Asked Questions

A few common questions we hear from Bay Area homeowners. For anything else, we reply to email within one business day.

What services do you offer across the San Francisco Bay Area?+
We're a full-service landscape company — design, construction, hardscape, irrigation, outdoor living, lighting, turf, fencing, tree work, and ongoing maintenance. Most of our projects are end-to-end, from the first site walk to the final walkthrough.
How long does a typical landscape project take?+
From first site visit to a finished build, most residential projects run 3–6 months. Permitting (when required) is often the wild card; on-site construction itself typically takes 6–14 weeks depending on scope.
Do you charge for a quote?+
No. Initial site visits and written quotes are free throughout our service area. For larger design-build projects we offer a paid design package — credited back to you if we build the project.
Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?+
Yes — California C-27 Landscape Contractor #1098432, with $2M general liability and full workers' compensation coverage for our seven-person crew. Documentation is available on request.
Do you pull permits?+
Yes. We handle permitting end-to-end for retaining walls over four feet, structural hardscape, pergolas, and any work requiring inspection. Permit fees are itemized separately in the quote.
Do you take on smaller projects?+
Absolutely. About a third of our work is smaller in scope — single patios, pergolas, retaining walls, outdoor kitchens, lighting upgrades. We batch smaller jobs so the crew stays efficient and the timing works for you.
