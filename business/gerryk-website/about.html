Our Story

Gerryk Landscaping Construction

Hello, my name is Gerryk and I'm the proud owner of Gerryk Landscaping. We have a team of highly skilled and experienced landscape contractors who are dedicated to providing top-quality commercial and residential construction services throughout the Bay Area, California.

Our landscape experts are trained to identify potential issues with your outdoor space — grading, drainage, existing hardscape, structural elements. From demolition through final install, we manage every stage of the build in-house.

We understand the significance of maintaining the integrity of your outdoor construction. Neglected hardscape, drainage and structural issues can significantly diminish the usability and safety of your property. Our comprehensive range of landscape construction services is built to keep your outdoor space safe, functional and looking sharp for decades.
