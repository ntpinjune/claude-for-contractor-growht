### Claude Managed Agents Memory (public beta) — Anthropic API Docs (fetched directly)

- **Link**: `https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/whats-new-claude-4-7` (fetched directly Apr 24)
- **Why it matters**: The jump to 3.75MP images (up from 1.15MP) plus 1:1 pixel coordinate mapping is a step-change for vision tasks that require spatial precision — damage assessment, schematic reading, document parsing

## Business angles for Noah

### 1. "Customer Memory" upsell for contractor CRMs
- **Trigger**: Claude Managed Agents Memory going public beta (Apr 23)
- **The play**: Build an AI layer on top of ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro/Jobber that maintains a persistent memory store per customer — system age, past issues, brand preferences, service history, even personality notes. When the homeowner calls, the dispatched tech or AI phone bot already knows "3-ton Carrier R-410A, compressor replaced 2024, customer prefers morning appointments." Package as a $49-99/month add-on for any contractor already using one of the major field-service platforms.
- **Why now**: The Managed Agents Memory API just hit public beta with a stable REST interface and full versioning — you could prototype this in a weekend without building a database
- **Contractor fit**: DIRECT — service contracts and repeat business are the profit engine for HVAC and plumbing; memory is literally the product differentiation

### 2. AI-powered field photo → instant estimate
- **Trigger**: Claude Opus 4.7 high-res vision (3.75MP, 1:1 pixel coords, Apr 16)
- **The play**: Mobile app for field techs: photograph the job site (roof damage, HVAC unit, plumbing under a sink), submit to Opus 4.7 with a system prompt that knows the contractor's labor rates and material costs, get back a structured draft estimate in under 30 seconds. Sell as a per-seat SaaS to roofing and HVAC companies struggling with estimate turnaround time.
- **Why now**: Opus 4.7's 3.75MP support with 1:1 pixel mapping is new this month — damage detection and parts identification from photos is now feasible at the resolution contractors actually shoot with iPhones
- **Contractor fit**: DIRECT — slow estimates are the #1 close-rate killer in storm-damage roofing and HVAC replacement sales

### 3. Outbound AI phone agent for subcontractor / supplier coordination
- **Trigger**: Ring-a-Ding OpenClaw at $19/month (Apr 18)
- **The play**: Contractors spend hours per week calling suppliers for parts availability, calling subs to confirm schedules, calling warranty departments to open claims. Wire Ring-a-Ding into a simple workflow tool: dispatcher drops job details into a form, AI makes the calls, transcripts flow back into the job record. First version can be a no-code Zapier/Make workflow; later becomes a native integration.
- **Why now**: Ring-a-Ding makes the telephony infra trivially cheap. The constraint was never the idea — it was the cost and complexity of the phone layer
- **Contractor fit**: DIRECT — dispatchers at mid-size contractors (5-20 techs) are the bottleneck, and most of their time is on exactly these coordination calls

### 4. GPT-5.5 back-office agent for multi-location contractors
- **Trigger**: GPT-5.5 launch (Apr 23), specifically its "navigating computer work" capability gains
- **The play**: Multi-location HVAC/plumbing/electrical businesses have a back-office mess: separate ServiceTitan instances or franchisee logins, inconsistent job costing, manual P&L roll-ups. An agent that can operate the software UI (log in, pull reports, normalize data, push it to a shared dashboard) becomes the integration layer that no one else wants to build. Sell as a monthly managed service rather than SaaS.
- **Why now**: GPT-5.5 is specifically better at operating software interfaces and "analyzing data, writing code, creating documents and spreadsheets" — exactly what multi-location back-office work requires
- **Contractor fit**: ADJACENT — most relevant for franchisors and multi-location groups (10+ locations), not the single-location contractor

### 5. Before/after marketing content generator using gpt-image-2
- **Trigger**: ChatGPT Images 2.0 launch (Apr 21), reliable text rendering + multi-image reasoning
- **The play**: Contractors take job-completion photos but almost none of them turn those photos into marketing content. A simple tool: upload before + after photos, AI generates a polished split-image with branded overlay, caption suggestions for Google Business Profile, and an SMS-ready version for the customer review request. gpt-image-2's reliable text rendering means the business name and contact info in the overlay won't be garbled.
- **Why now**: gpt-image-2 is the first image model that can reliably render correct text in images — previous models made branded overlays unusable without manual correction
- **Contractor fit**: ADJACENT — strong fit for roofers and remodelers who already document with photos; weaker for HVAC where the "after" isn't photogenic
